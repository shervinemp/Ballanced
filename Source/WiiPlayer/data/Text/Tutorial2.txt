Game Tutorial:
Press + at any time to pause or quit the game.
Press A to continue the tutorial, or - to skip it.
*View Rotation:
Press left or right on the +Control Pad to turn the view in 90° steps.

Continue with A ...
*Overview:
Hold up on the +Control Pad to raise the viewpoint.

Continue with A ...
*Navigation:
Turn the view until the track with the two towers lies ahead of you.
Tilt the Wii Remote, held sideways, to roll the ball (Nunchuk: Control Stick). Down on the +Control Pad makes the way you hold it level.

Follow the path!
*Extra Life:
Falling off the track costs you a life. The lives you have left are shown at the bottom right of the screen. Collect an Extra Life to gain another one.

Continue with A ...
*Transformers:
Transformers change what your ball is made of. The surface of the transformer field shows which material you will get. This is a stone transformer.

Continue with A ...
*This slope is too steep for a stone ball!
Find a wood transformer and try again.
Tip: turn the view now and then (left/right on the +Control Pad) to find your way. Careful when rolling backwards!

Continue with A ...
*Extra Points:
The counter on the left shows your time points, which keep running down as you play. Collect Extra Points to add to your score.

Continue with A ...
*Checkpoints:
This is the first checkpoint on your way. If you lose a life, you continue from the last checkpoint you passed.

Continue with A ...
*One last thing:
Press HOME at any time for the HOME Menu, where you can return to the Homebrew Channel or the Wii Menu.

Leave the tutorial with A ...
